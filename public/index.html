The auction

One price,
many winners.

A sealed batch auction with a uniform clearing price. Bids are collected privately for a fixed window, then cleared all at once. Everyone who bid at least the final price gets goods, and all pay the same price.

01

Collect sealed bids

Quantity + maximum price. Money is reserved up front. Nobody sees other orders.

02

Find one price

Sort by price, ignore arrival time. The price where demand first covers supply is p*.

03

Fill & share

Above p*: filled in full. At p*: pro-rata, whole tonnes, ties by a pre-locked lottery.

04

Pay & verify

Pay tonnes × p* + 0.1%. The rest is refunded. Anyone can recompute the round.

Fair & safe

The exchange checks
the limit, not the AI.

The AI never sets prices. A deterministic engine with whole-number math does, and it checks these rules after every round. They are tested on all 24 orderings of the same orders and on 5,000 random markets.

Total sold ≤ supplyThe seller never oversells
Each buyer gets ≤ what they askedNobody is forced to take more
Everyone pays the same p*No price discrimination
Nobody pays above their maximumYour limit is always respected
Arrival order doesn't matterSpeed and timing give no advantage
Published seed hash → reveal → VerifyEvery round is auditable, SHA-256 fingerprinted

Honest limits

  • A very large buyer can sometimes lower the price by bidding below their true value. Batch size and reserve prices limit this.
  • A buyer at the cutoff may get only part of what they asked for. The agent says so before they bid.
  • By default the agent proposes and you confirm. Full autonomy is opt-in, with small limits.
  • This is a prototype: rounds and other bidders are simulated, and prices come from real UZEX data.
